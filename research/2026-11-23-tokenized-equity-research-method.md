# Public research-method note: tokenized equities and price discovery
November 23, 2026. General educational methodology only; not investment advice, trading signals, account-specific information or a backtest result.

A security represented in tokenized form and a separate stock-backed token can differ in legal rights, custody, redemption and trading venues. Compare the actual instruments and eligibility, not just ticker labels. See the SEC's March 2026 Nasdaq order and September 2026 conditional innovation exemption:
- https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/self-regulatory-organization-rulemaking/sr-nasdaq-2025-072
- https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2026-90-sec-issues-innovation-exemption-facilitate-trading-tokenized-nms-stock-request-comment

For research on market-price discrepancies, record instrument identity, issuer, chain, pool/venue, source and observation timestamp, stock-market session, executable bid and ask at the intended size, liquidity, spreads, fees, and settlement/custody constraints. Aggregated historical prices are not executable orders. Compare same-time returns, alternative benchmarks, and out-of-sample evidence rather than assuming price correlation implies prediction or a stale quote implies arbitrage. Include unsuccessful and missed signals, uncertainty, and realistic execution costs.
